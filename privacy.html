Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 20, 2026

Useful Tech & Home Gear respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled in connection with our website and our Pinterest integration.

Pinterest API Integration

Useful Tech & Home Gear uses the Pinterest API to manage and analyze content associated with our own Pinterest business account.

The integration may access information made available through the Pinterest API, including Pins, boards, account-related content and analytics data, only as authorized by Pinterest and the account owner.

How We Use Information

Information obtained through the Pinterest API is used only for internal business purposes, including organizing published content, monitoring performance, maintaining product-linked content, improving publishing workflows, and supporting content management decisions.

Data Storage and Security

Data used by our internal automation tools is stored only as necessary to support our content management and monitoring workflows. We take reasonable measures to protect authentication credentials, access tokens, and other sensitive information from unauthorized access.

Pinterest access tokens, application secrets and other credentials are not published on this website or intentionally exposed in public code.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information or Pinterest account data. Information accessed through the Pinterest API is not shared with unrelated third parties except where required by law or necessary to operate authorized technical services supporting our internal workflow.

Data Retention and Deletion

Data is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for our internal content management, analytics and monitoring purposes.

Requests regarding access, correction or deletion of information associated with this integration may be submitted through our Contact page.

Third-Party Services

Our activities may involve third-party services including Pinterest, Amazon and GitHub. Their own privacy policies and terms apply to their respective platforms and services.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services, integrations or legal requirements change. The latest version will always be published on this page.

Contact

For privacy-related questions or requests, please use our Contact page.
